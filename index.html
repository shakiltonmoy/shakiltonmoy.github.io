Current Projects

🧬
Ongoing · PhD
Evolutionary Trajectories of Organelle-Derived DNA in Fabaceae Nuclear Genomes
Investigating how DNA from chloroplasts and mitochondria integrates into the nuclear genomes of legume plants (Fabaceae). Using bioinformatics pipelines on HPC clusters to detect, classify, and characterize NUMTs (nuclear mitochondrial DNA) and NUPTs (nuclear plastid DNA) across dozens of species, revealing patterns of organelle-to-nucleus gene transfer throughout plant evolution. This work builds directly on my MS thesis and now extends to a comparative genomics framework at the PhD level.
NUMT / NUPT Detection minimap2 BLAST Genome Assembly HTCondor HPC Python R Fabaceae
🔬 Bailey Lab · NMSU · Sep 2023 – Present

Plant Response to Electrical Treatments & Secondary Metabolites
Examining whether low-current electricity exposure affects secondary metabolite production in plants. Combines HPLC analysis, Total Phenolics extraction, and metabolomics to understand electrochemical stress responses — a novel intersection of plant physiology and agricultural biotechnology.
HPLC Metabolomics Plant Physiology Total Phenolics

Phytoremediation of Cadmium & Arsenic in Contaminated Soils
Investigated the capacity of Brassica species and Pteris vittata (brake fern) to extract toxic heavy metals (cadmium and arsenic) from contaminated agricultural soils. Includes heavy metal extraction protocols and analytical chemistry techniques.

05 / About

My Story

"From rice paddies
to DNA sequencers —
one curious mind."
I grew up in Bangladesh, where agricultural landscapes shaped my love for the natural world. At the University of Rajshahi, I pursued a BSc in Agriculture, discovering the power of science to explain how living systems work — and earning a scholarship for four consecutive years.

In 2023, I crossed an ocean and a continent to join New Mexico State University. My MS research focused on how organelle DNA integrates into plant nuclear genomes, blending wet lab work with bioinformatics on HPC clusters. I graduated with a 3.768 GPA and presented at conferences at Santa Fe, NM and San Diego, CA.

Today, as a PhD student in Molecular Biology, I serve at NMSU's Genome Sequencing Facility, continue teaching, mentoring, and pushing the frontiers of plant genomics. I'm driven by curiosity, collaboration, and the belief that plants hold secrets worth decoding.
